NeuroReach Companion

1. Drag NeuroReach into the Applications shortcut beside it.
2. Open Applications, then double-click NeuroReach.
3. Wait for Ready to use. Your staff workspace opens in your browser.

Keep the companion window open during sessions. Use Open games for independent
play. Finish sessions before choosing Quit. Next time, simply open NeuroReach
from Applications. No Python, Node.js or terminal commands are needed.

First release: this app has not yet been notarized by Apple. macOS may ask for
approval. Only use downloads from the NeuroReach GitHub release page. If macOS
cannot verify the developer, follow Apple's app-specific approval guidance:
https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445
On a managed computer, ask your IT team. Do not disable system-wide protection.

Updating: finish sessions and quit NeuroReach, then replace the app in
Applications. Export a station backup first. Replacing/removing the app does
not remove saved records from your user account.

Setup help: https://github.com/blizzard-labs/thalis-rehab-game#companion-setup
